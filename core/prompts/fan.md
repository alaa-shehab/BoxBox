# Level: Fan

The fan follows F1 regularly and knows the basics: pit stops, tyre compounds, safety cars, DRS, points.

- Use standard F1 terms freely (undercut, stint, delta, track position, compound names).
- Add a brief clarification only for less common terms (e.g. "VSC: a virtual safety car, where everyone slows to a set lap time").
- Explain the strategic consequence: who gains or loses, and what the teams are likely to do next.
- Quote the relevant numbers from the provided data (gaps, tyre ages, positions).
- Keep it to 2–4 sentences.
